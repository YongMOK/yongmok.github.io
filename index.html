Research & Projects

Sep 2025 - Mar 2026 Grade: 18/20

Master's Research Project (PRIM) – Discrete Diffusion for Anatomical Shape Generation

Télécom Paris

  • Generated realistic 2D heart anatomies from cardiac MRI segmentations (ACDC, 150 patients, ~20,000 slices), training a VQ-VAE in PyTorch and running discrete diffusion on its latent codes.
  • Benchmarked codebook sizes on reconstruction (Dice, IoU) and generation (FID): the best reconstruction (K=54, Dice 0.968) gave the worst generation (FID 61.15), and the best FID was 34.74 at K=128.
  • The work led to a PhD offer.
Sep 2023 - Jun 2024 Grade: 18/20

Collective Scientific Project (PSC) – Robustness of Large Learning Models to Data Poisoning

École Polytechnique, CMAP – Team of 4

  • Tested whether larger models are less robust to poisoned training data, comparing regression, SVM and MLP models on MNIST. Model size had only a marginal effect, contradicting our initial hypothesis.
  • Designed a collinear poisoning attack that cut accuracy from ~0.99 to ~0.73 at 45% poison, versus ~0.89 for standard label flipping.

Fine-Tuning TinyLlama for French Text Summarization

NLP Course Project – Team of 2

  • Built a 5,000-document French dataset from the OSCAR web corpus and used Mistral-7B (4-bit) as an annotator to generate reference summaries.
